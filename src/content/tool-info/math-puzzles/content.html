Math Puzzles

Workflow

Choose the puzzle settings, then Generate.

Review and output

Review the puzzle and solution pages before printing or exporting.

Use Fit page to see a complete page, or Fit width to read and scroll. On smaller screens, Settings opens the controls and Guide switches this pane.

Paper size

Statement supports grids up to 18 by 18 while retaining the selected difficulty. Very large values may not fit the cells; reduce the number ranges or choose Letter if the fit warning appears.
