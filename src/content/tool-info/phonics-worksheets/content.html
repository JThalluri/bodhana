Review and output

The settings tabs select options. Guide is separate from those tabs. Review the pages before printing or exporting.

Use Fit page to see a complete page, or Fit width to read and scroll. On smaller screens, Settings opens the controls and Guide switches this pane.

American pronunciation

Enough sounds like uh-NUF: ou supplies the cup vowel and gh supplies /f/. Glacier uses GLAY-sher: a has the day vowel, ci sounds like sh, and er is the final r-colored vowel. Both words have two syllables.

Dictionary Builder Detail shows these complete sound correspondences. Existing imported CSV analyses stay as supplied; use Source > Refresh analysis to apply the current pronunciation rules to a previously loaded list.
