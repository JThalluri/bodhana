Phonics Worksheets

Workflow

Load a word list, choose an activity and adjust its settings, then inspect the automatically updated worksheet.

Review and output

The settings tabs select options. Guide is separate from those tabs. Review the pages before printing or exporting.

Use Fit page to see a complete page, or Fit width to read and scroll. On smaller screens, Settings opens the controls and Guide switches this pane.

American pronunciation

Enough sounds like uh-NUF: ou supplies the cup vowel and gh supplies /f/. Glacier uses GLAY-sher: a has the day vowel, ci sounds like sh, and er is the final r-colored vowel. Both words have two syllables.

Dictionary Builder Detail shows these complete sound correspondences. Existing imported CSV analyses stay as supplied; use Source > Refresh analysis to apply the current pronunciation rules to a previously loaded list.

Offline sound guides and online exploration

Every enriched word in Dictionary Builder Detail uses the same sound-guide layout. Ordinary words use phonics rules; unresolved parts are marked Needs review. A spelling group can contain several sounds, so group counts are not always phoneme counts.

Explore pronunciation (online) opens Cambridge Dictionary in a new tab for sound-by-sound guidance and audio. Choose its US pronunciation. Internet is required only for that external reference; the portal's local sound guides, saved words and worksheets continue to work offline, including the portable HTML file.

Paper size

Choose Letter or Statement in Activity > Paper size. Words per page is a maximum: tall rows continue onto additional pages while the writing boxes retain their size.
