Handwriting Lines

Workflow

Choose the writing style and enter text using the settings.

Review and output

Review line placement and all generated pages before printing.

Use Fit page to see a complete page, or Fit width to read and scroll. On smaller screens, Settings opens the controls and Guide switches this pane.

Word Practice

In Setup, choose Format > Word Practice. Enter words in Content or load a local dictionary and add selected words. Words run left to right in three columns, then continue on the next row and page.

Choose Words only for an underlined word list, or Practice lines for one to five writing rows below each word row. Full page makes blank ruling and keeps your word list for later. Lines controls choose primary ruling or a single underline, line height, gaps and colors. Text controls reuse the local fonts, letter spacing and font size (8–44 px for Word Practice). Long words wrap within their column.

Paper size

Setup offers US Letter (8.5 × 11 inches) and Statement (5.5 × 8.5 inches) for every Handwriting Lines format. Letter remains the default. Layout margins apply to the selected paper; 0.25-inch margins leave more room for three-column word practice. Preview fitting changes only screen size. Print and Export PDF use the actual selected paper dimensions. Select matching paper and 100% / Actual size in the printer dialog; check a physical print before classroom use.
