Math Games

Workflow

Choose the game and its settings, then Generate.

Review and output

Review every generated page before printing or exporting.

Use Fit page to see a complete page, or Fit width to read and scroll. On smaller screens, Settings opens the controls and Guide switches this pane.

Paper size

Statement supports 1, 2 or 4 games per page. Two and four use compact boards; choose one for larger writing spaces. Letter also supports six and eight. Large custom numbers or font sizes may require fewer games. Bingo call sheets and Match answer keys continue onto additional pages as needed.
