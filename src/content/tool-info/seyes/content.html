Seyes Writing

Workflow

Enter text and adjust the writing settings. The preview updates as you work.

Review and output

Print through this module’s dedicated Print action. Choose Letter or Statement in Paper size and use matching stock at actual size; validate the physical grid on your printer.

Use Fit page to see a complete page, or Fit width to read and scroll. On smaller screens, Settings opens the controls and Guide switches this pane.

Changing paper size changes the page bounds and text pagination. The major grid remains 8 mm with 2 mm minor rules. Long words continue within the smaller page width.
