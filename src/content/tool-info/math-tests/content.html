Math Tests

Workflow

Choose arithmetic operations and ranges, then Generate.

Review and output

Adjust the question count and page settings before generating. Use Print or Export to save the generated pages.

Use Fit page to see a complete page, or Fit width to read and scroll. On smaller screens, Settings opens the controls and Guide switches this pane.

Paper size

Choose Letter or Statement in Paper size. Statement continues each test on additional pages, keeping every question and its number.
