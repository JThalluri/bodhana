Word Puzzles

Workflow

Choose a puzzle type and provide the word list, then Generate.

Review and output

Check dense puzzles and any solution pages before printing.

Use Fit page to see a complete page, or Fit width to read and scroll. On smaller screens, Settings opens the controls and Guide switches this pane.

Paper size

Statement supports new grids up to 20 by 20. Grid letters fit the stock; long word banks and jumbles continue onto additional pages.
