Certificates

Workflow

Choose a template and enter the student name, message, date and issuer.

Review and output

Paper size selects landscape Letter or Statement. Shorten text if the overflow warning appears, then Print. Statement has less text space; printing is unavailable until the content fits.

Use Fit page to see a complete page, or Fit width to read and scroll. On smaller screens, Settings opens the controls and Guide switches this pane.
